document-service-py

A from-scratch viewer against the Python/FastAPI port.

Any file is converted to PDF for viewing: PDF, Word/RTF/ODT/text/HTML, Excel/CSV, PowerPoint, and images (PNG, JPEG, TIFF incl. multi-page, BMP, GIF, WEBP). Scanned pages are OCR'd so their text is searchable.
